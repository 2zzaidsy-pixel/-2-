## The Iceberg Model of Behavior

Think of behavior as an iceberg:

- **Above water** (10%): Your conscious actions and decisions
- **Below water** (90%): Beliefs, values, past experiences, biological drives, social conditioning

Most attempts at change fail because they focus only on the visible 10%.

## The Three Layers of Human Motivation

### 1. Biological Layer

We are biological organisms before we are thinking beings. Sleep, hunger, hormonesâ€”all of these dramatically influence our behavior in ways we rarely acknowledge.

### 2. Psychological Layer

Your personality traits, cognitive patterns, emotional regulation, and mental health all shape how you respond to the world. This is where therapy, coaching, and self-development work.

### 3. Social Layer

We are deeply social creatures. Family, culture, peer groups, and societal norms create powerful invisible forces that guide our behavior.

## The Behavior Change Formula

```text
B = f(P, E)
```

Behavior is a function of the Person and their Environment.

To change your behavior, you can:

1. **Change the person**: Build new skills, beliefs, and identities
2. **Change the environment**: Design your surroundings to make good choices easier

## Practical Applications

### For Self-Change

- Identify the **cue** that triggers your habit
- Make the desired behavior **easy** (reduce friction)
- Make the undesired behavior **hard** (increase friction)
- Celebrate small wins to **reward** the new behavior

### For Understanding Others

- Ask "why" five times to get to root causes
- Consider the context before judging someone's actions
- Remember that everyone is doing their best with their current resources
